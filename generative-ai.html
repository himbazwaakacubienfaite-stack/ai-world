Generative AI

Discover how AI can create new content such as text, images, music and more.

What Is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that can create new content based on patterns learned from data.

It can produce text, images, audio, video and other forms of content from instructions given by users.

What Can It Create?

Text

AI can generate stories, explanations, summaries and other written content.

Images

AI can generate images from written descriptions.

Audio

Some AI systems can generate or transform music and speech.

Video

Generative AI can create or help edit videos from instructions and existing content.

How Does Generative AI Work?

Generative AI learns patterns from large amounts of data and uses those patterns to create new content.

Learn from Data

AI models are trained using large amounts of information and examples.

Recognize Patterns

The model learns relationships and patterns within the information it receives.

Generate Content

When given an instruction, the model uses what it learned to generate new content.
