Benefits & Challenges of AI

AI offers many possibilities, but it also brings important challenges.

Benefits of AI

Faster Work

AI can process large amounts of information quickly.

Innovation

AI can help people explore ideas and develop new solutions.

Learning Support

AI tools can provide explanations, examples and practice opportunities.

Challenges of AI

Errors

AI systems can sometimes produce incorrect or misleading information.

Privacy

AI systems can involve important questions about data and privacy.

Human Responsibility

People still need to make important decisions and use AI responsibly.
