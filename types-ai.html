Types of Artificial Intelligence

AI can be understood in different ways, depending on its abilities and how it learns.

Major AI Concepts

ANI

Artificial Narrow Intelligence is designed to perform specific tasks.

Machine Learning

A method that allows computers to learn patterns from data.

Deep Learning

A type of machine learning that uses multi-layered neural networks.

Generative AI

AI that can generate new content such as text, images and audio.

AGI

Artificial General Intelligence is a theoretical form of AI with broad abilities.
