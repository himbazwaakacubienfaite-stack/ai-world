AI Applications

Artificial intelligence is being used in many areas of modern life.

Where Is AI Used?

Medicine

AI can help analyze medical images, research information and patient data.

Education

AI can support students with explanations, practice and personalized learning.

Agriculture

AI can help monitor crops, weather conditions and agricultural data.

Transport

AI is used in navigation, traffic management and vehicle technologies.

Science

AI can help researchers analyze large amounts of scientific data.

Business

Businesses use AI for analysis, automation, customer support and other tasks.
